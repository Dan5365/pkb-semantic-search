# Survey Results (aggregated)

**Project:** PKB Semantic Search
**Instrument:** Google Form, Russian language, October 2026
**Respondents:** 8. Contact data is not stored in this document. Raw responses are kept locally and are not committed.

## 1. Method and limitations

- Convenience sample of acquaintances of the team; not representative.
- The form used fixed-choice answers for questions 5, 6, 7 and 10, which differs from the open-text draft in `docs/interviews/survey.md`. The draft is to be updated to match the form as sent.
- Question 8 was answered by 7 respondents. Question 9 (tool and quality) was answered by 7 respondents, but only 3 of them reported having used semantic search. The reason is under review and these answers are reported as given, without interpretation.
- Results are descriptive. No statistical inference is made.

## 2. Results

**Primary note tool (Q1, n=8):** Apple Notes 5, Google Keep 2, Notion 1.

**Number of notes (Q2, n=8):** up to 100: 6; 100–500: 1; 500–2000: 1.

**Search frequency (Q3, n=8):** every day 1; several times a week 4; once a week 3; rarely 0.

**How the note is found (Q4, multiple choice, n=8):** keyword search 4; folders and tags 3; scrolling the list 3; links between notes 2; recently opened notes 1.

**Last search episode (Q5, n=8):** searched by title 3; browsed folders 3; found through related notes 2.

**Unable to find a note that is known to exist (Q6, n=8):** sometimes 3; rarely 3; never 2.

**Strategy when exact words are not remembered (Q7, n=8):** search by individual keywords 5; try to recall other words from the note 3.

**Experience with search by meaning (Q8, n=7):** heard of it, never used 3; tried a few times 2; use regularly 1; did not know about it 1.

**Tool and quality (Q9, n=7, see limitation above):** Notion, works, sometimes returns irrelevant results 4; Obsidian, works well, finds the right notes quickly 3.

**Perceived usefulness of search by meaning (Q10, n=8):** very useful 4; somewhat useful 1; not particularly useful 2; not useful at all 1.

**Description of ideal search (Q11, n=8):** finds notes by meaning without exact words 4; quickly returns the most relevant results 3; keyword, tag and date filters 1.

## 3. Facts and assumptions

**Facts from the answers (as reported):**
- Most respondents keep fewer than 100 notes and use Apple Notes.
- Keyword search and browsing are the most common ways to find a note.
- Half of respondents say they sometimes or rarely fail to find a note they know exists.
- Most respondents who expressed an opinion consider search by meaning useful.

**Assumptions (not confirmed by this sample):**
- That the same problems appear for users of Notion or Obsidian as main tools; the sample has too few such respondents.
- That the reported usefulness of search by meaning predicts actual use.

## 4. Implications for the study

The sample supports the problem statement qualitatively: people do fail to find known notes and would value search by meaning. It does not support any claim about which retrieval strategy performs better. That question is answered only by the labelled evaluation set (PKB-15).
